Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my booking?

Yes, you can change your booking up to 24 hours before departure at no extra cost. Contact our support team for assistance.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 48 hours before departure are refunded in full. Cancellations within 48 hours receive 50% refund.

Are pets allowed on buses?

Service animals are always welcome. For other pets, please contact us at support@travelbus.com for special arrangements.

Do you offer group discounts?

Yes! Groups of 10 or more receive a 15% discount. Contact our group sales team at groups@travelbus.com
