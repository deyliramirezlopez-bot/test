About TravelBus

Our Story

Founded in 2010, TravelBus has been committed to providing affordable, safe, and comfortable bus travel across the United States. What started as a single route serving 100 passengers has grown into a nationwide network serving over 500,000 passengers annually.

Our Mission

To make intercity travel accessible to everyone by providing affordable, reliable, and comfortable bus services with exceptional customer service.
